Cricket is one of the most popular sports in the world.
It is played between two teams of eleven players each.
A cricket match is played on a field with a central pitch.
The main formats of cricket are Test, ODI, and T20.
The team that scores more runs usually wins the match.
A batsman tries to score runs by hitting the ball.
A bowler tries to dismiss the batsman and restrict runs.
Fielders help their team by catching the ball and stopping runs.
A wicket can fall in several ways, including bowled and caught.
An over consists of six legal deliveries.
The captain plays an important role in making team decisions.
Cricket requires skill, patience, fitness, and teamwork.
Test cricket is the longest traditional format of the game.
One Day International matches usually have fifty overs per side.
T20 cricket is a shorter and fast-paced format with twenty overs.
The Cricket World Cup is one of the sport's biggest tournaments.
Pakistan has produced many talented cricketers over the years.
Batting and bowling strategies can change depending on pitch conditions.
Umpires make important decisions during a cricket match.
Cricket continues to attract millions of fans around the world.
